Undergraduate, Dept. of Mechanical System Engineering (Intelligent Robotics)

I register self-collected six-camera driving data with COLMAP and add depth and normal supervision to train geometrically reliable 3DGS scenes. This portfolio documents how that work was extended into a pipeline for closed-loop evaluation of autonomous driving algorithms inside those scenes.
